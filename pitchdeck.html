Resolution 05 / 14

Objective rug criteria — code decides, not humans

Resolution is driven by verifiable on-chain and market data. Any one condition triggers RUG.

  • Dev dump — Creator wallet sells >25% of holdings (Helius transaction history)
  • Price collapse — Token drops >90% from market-open price (DexScreener)
  • Liquidity pull — Liquidity removed >80% vs open snapshot (DexScreener)
  • Failed graduation — Token fails to graduate after reaching defined bonding-curve stage (roadmap)

Survive condition

Market duration elapses with zero rug triggers.

Backend resolver (BullMQ, 30s) → resolve_market on-chain → winners claim via claim_payout.

Early rug resolution supported on-chain (expiry gate removed for MVP).

Trust model Automated · Auditable · No manual outcome votes

Adjacent, not a direct competitor

General prediction markets — politics, sports, macro. Not memecoin-native. No flow to paste an existing Pump.fun mint and bet SURVIVE vs RUG in a 1h/6h/24h window. Resolution is human- or oracle-led, not tuned for per-token rug signals on Solana.

The Solana memecoin launchpad with prediction markets on every token they launch. Launch a coin in seconds. Bet YES/NO on whether it rugs, moons, or graduates to Raydium. Markets resolve automatically from on-chain signals — profit from calling a rug, not just being early.
